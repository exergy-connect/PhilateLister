Greater Houston Stamp Show

Auction Rules

Saturday 19 and Sunday 20 September 2026

Auction open

Saturday 19 & Sunday 20 September

Bidding closes

Sunday 20 September at 2:00 PM

Registration

  1. Participants must sign the registration sheet to receive a unique bid number.
  2. By signing, participants acknowledge that they have read and will follow these rules.
  3. Participants may optionally provide a phone number so they can be notified if they win an item.

Bidding

  1. Bids are written on the bid sheet for each item.
  2. Only one person may access the auction binder at a time.
  3. Each item has a minimum bid, as marked on its bid sheet.
  4. All bids must be in $5 increments.
  5. Once submitted, bids cannot be withdrawn.
  6. The auction committee determines the winning bids, interpreting handwriting as accurately as possible.

Closing & Payment

  1. The auction closes Sunday, 20 September 2026 at 2:00 PM.
  2. At closing, the highest bid on each item is the winning bid.
  3. Winning bids preferably paid in cash, or a check made out to GHSS Foundation.
  4. A winning bidder may pay and retrieve their item after the auction closes.

Unclaimed Items

  1. By 3:00 PM Sunday, 20 September, if an item has not been retrieved, the next-highest bid from a different bidder is selected, and that bidder will be notified.
  2. If either of the two highest bidders comes to claim the item after 3:00 PM, they may pay their bid price and retrieve the item. Any items still unclaimed when the show closes at 4:00 PM Sunday remain the property of GHSS.
