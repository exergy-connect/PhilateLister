# GHSS Auction Rules

Greater Houston Stamp Show · Saturday 19 and Sunday 20 September 2026

The auction runs throughout the show. Bidding closes **Sunday, 20 September 2026 at 2:00 PM**.

## Registration

1. Participants must sign the registration sheet to receive a unique bid number.
2. By signing, participants acknowledge that they have read and will follow these rules.
3. Participants may optionally provide a phone number so they can be notified if they win an item.

## Bidding

4. Bids are written on the bid sheet for each item.
5. Only one person may access the auction binder at a time.
6. Each item has a minimum bid, as marked on its bid sheet.
7. All bids must be in **$5 increments**.
8. Once submitted, bids **cannot be withdrawn**.
9. The auction committee determines the winning bids, interpreting handwriting as accurately as possible.

## Closing & Payment

10. The auction closes **Sunday, 20 September 2026 at 2:00 PM**.
11. At closing, the highest bid on each item is the winning bid.
12. Winning bids preferably paid **in cash**, or a check made out to GHSS Foundation.
13. A winning bidder may pay and retrieve their item after the auction closes.

## Unclaimed Items

14. By **3:00 PM Sunday, 20 September**, if an item has not been retrieved, the next-highest bid from a different bidder is selected, and that bidder will be notified.
15. If either of the two highest bidders comes to claim the item after 3:00 PM, they may pay their bid price and retrieve the item. Any items still unclaimed when the show closes at **4:00 PM Sunday remain the property of GHSS**.
